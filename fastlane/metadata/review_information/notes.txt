Ondo is a player only: it does not host, provide or sell any content or IPTV subscription, ships no channels or playlists, and does not link to or promote any provider. Users add the M3U playlist or the Xtream Codes login given by their own provider. There is no sign-in, no account, no in-app purchase and no advertising. The screenshots show a generated demo catalogue (drawn posters, fictional channels).

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT AN IPTV SUBSCRIPTION
1. Launch the app. The first screen is "Welcome to Ondo".
2. Tap "Try the demo" (the button below "Add a Playlist"). This adds a playlist named "Demo" that loads https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/fr.m3u, a list of publicly available channels maintained by the open-source iptv-org project (https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv). Ondo is not affiliated with it.
3. Open Live TV (tab bar on iPhone, sidebar on iPad and Mac) and choose a channel. On the Mac the player opens in its own window: use the Full Screen button or the F key for full screen.
The demo list contains live channels only, so Movies and Series stay empty: they fill in when a provider's playlist includes movies and series. Some channels may be offline or limited to their country; any other channel in the list can be used.

WHY NSAllowsArbitraryLoads
IPTV providers very often serve their playlists, Xtream Codes API, TV guides (XMLTV), logos and streams over plain HTTP, from servers the user types in and the app cannot know in advance, so exception domains cannot be listed. The demo list also contains HTTP streams. NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsForMedia alone is not enough: playlists, the API, guides and logos are not media requests, and adding it next to NSAllowsArbitraryLoads makes the system ignore NSAllowsArbitraryLoads. The app only connects to the servers the user adds, to TMDb if the user enters a TMDb key, and to subtitle services when the user searches for subtitles.

PERMISSIONS, BACKGROUND MODE AND ENTITLEMENTS
- Local network (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription): asked the first time the user taps the Cast button (to find Chromecast and AirPlay devices), or plays a stream served from a server on the user's own network.
- Notifications: only for the programme reminders the user sets; asked with the first reminder.
- UIBackgroundModes audio: Picture in Picture, and the sound of the stream being watched, which can go on when the app goes to the background.
- AirPlay (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and Chromecast (iPhone, iPad) play the user's own streams on a TV or projector.
- Mac sandbox: com.apple.security.network.client for the servers the user adds, TMDb and the subtitle services; com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write for the playlist and subtitle files the user picks and the backups the user saves or restores; com.apple.security.network.server for one listener bound to 127.0.0.1 only: an MKV movie is handed to the system player (AVPlayer) as HLS served on the loopback address, its tracks copied into fragmented MP4 as the player asks for them. Nothing outside the Mac can connect to it.
