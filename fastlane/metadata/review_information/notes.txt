Ondo is a player only: it does not host, provide or sell any content or IPTV subscription, ships no channels or playlists, and does not link to or promote any provider. Users add the M3U playlist or the Xtream Codes login given by their own provider. There is no sign-in, no account and no advertising; the only in-app purchases are three optional tips, which unlock nothing (below). The screenshots show a demo catalogue of open-licensed films and fictional channels.

HOW TO TEST WITHOUT AN IPTV SUBSCRIPTION
1. Launch the app. The first screen is "Welcome to Ondo".
2. Tap "Try the demo" (the button below "Add a Playlist"). This adds a playlist named "Demo" that loads https://iptv-org.github.io/iptv/countries/fr.m3u, a list of publicly available channels maintained by the open-source iptv-org project (https://github.com/iptv-org/iptv). Ondo is not affiliated with it.
3. Open Live TV (tab bar on iPhone, sidebar on iPad and Mac) and choose a channel. On the Mac the player opens in its own window (F key: full screen).
The demo list has live channels only (Movies and Series fill in with a provider's playlist). Some channels may be offline or limited to their country; any other one can be used.

WHY NSAllowsArbitraryLoads
IPTV providers very often serve their playlists, Xtream Codes API, TV guides (XMLTV), logos and streams over plain HTTP, from servers the user types in and the app cannot know in advance, so exception domains cannot be listed. The demo list also contains HTTP streams. NSAllowsArbitraryLoadsForMedia is not enough: playlists, the API, guides and logos are not media requests. The app connects to the servers the user adds (and the public demo list when "Try the demo" is tapped), once a week to GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com) for the public awards file, which the user can turn off in Settings, to TMDb if the user enters a TMDb key, and to subtitle services when the user searches for subtitles.

PERMISSIONS, BACKGROUND MODE AND ENTITLEMENTS
- Local network (NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription): asked the first time the user taps the Cast button (to find Chromecast and AirPlay devices), plays a stream served from a server on the user's own network, or turns on Sync on this network (below).
- Notifications: only for the programme reminders the user sets; asked with the first reminder.
- UIBackgroundModes audio: Picture in Picture, and the sound of the stream being watched in the background.
- AirPlay (iPhone, iPad, Mac) and Chromecast (iPhone, iPad) play the user's own streams on a TV or projector.
- Mac sandbox: com.apple.security.network.client for the servers the user adds, the awards file, TMDb, the subtitle services and the sync sessions the app opens; com.apple.security.files.user-selected.read-write for the playlist and subtitle files the user picks and the backups the user saves or restores. Nothing else: the app listens for no incoming connection.

SYNC ON THIS NETWORK (OPTIONAL, OFF BY DEFAULT)
Settings → Sync on this network keeps playlists, My List, collections, progress and the TMDb token in step with Ondo on the user's other devices on the same local network, while the app is open on both; off until the user turns it on. The app is a client only: it browses Bonjour for the _ondo._tcp service (NSBonjourServices) that those devices advertise, connects to them, and sends nothing until it has checked that the address reached is a local network address (private, link-local or loopback); it never advertises a service and accepts no incoming connection. Nothing goes through the internet. Testing it needs a second device running Ondo with sync on.

TIPS (OPTIONAL IN-APP PURCHASES)
Settings → Leave a tip: three consumable In-App Purchases, com.markedoe.ondo.tip.small, com.markedoe.ondo.tip.medium and com.markedoe.ondo.tip.large. A tip unlocks nothing: the app is the same with or without one. Each is finished once paid: nothing to restore. No other way to pay or donate is offered.
