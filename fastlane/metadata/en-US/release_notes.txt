• On iPhone and iPad, MKV movies and episodes play with the system player when it can decode them: HDR10, HLG and Dolby Vision show, Dolby Atmos plays and Picture in Picture works. VLC plays the others.
• Player, More, Report a Problem…: a report of the playback (start and seek times, freezes and their cause, statistics; no stream address, login or title) to copy, share or send in a GitHub issue.
• Movies and series start faster and come back sooner after a skip or a jump; the picture follows your finger while you drag the time bar.
• Settings, Player: choose the buffer for live TV and for movies & series (Automatic, or 1 to 10 seconds). A longer buffer hides longer interruptions from the provider.
• With VLC, live channels freeze less: the buffer grows by itself after the provider's stream stops for a moment, and Stream Statistics say whether a freeze comes from the network or the player.
• Faster and smoother everywhere: large playlists open sooner, search works in the background, long lists scroll without stutter, and the TV guide opens at once with thousands of channels.
• Mac: full screen, from the button, the F key or a double-click; AirPlay sends the video to your TV, not only the sound.
• Resume starts exactly where you stopped, and a pause made while a movie opens stays paused.
• Find subtitles online from the player: OpenSubtitles, SubDL and SubSource with your own key, Addic7ed for series without a key.
• Ondo now speaks Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic and Hindi, as well as English and French.
• My List is now a tab on iPhone, with your movies, series and channels in one place; your own lists are now called collections.
• Tap an actor, a director, a genre, the year or an award on a movie or series page to see the titles of your playlist that share it; search finds actors and directors by name.
• Search can be filtered by release years, and the grids sort by release date.
• Hide a whole language or country in one go (category names that start with |PT|, FR:…), a style (manga & anime, children, documentaries…) or any word of category names; the categories added later follow.
• Awards and festival selections (Cannes, Oscars, Berlin, Venice, Césars…) on movie pages, an Award Winners row, and a search filter by festival or ceremony.
• Home shows when the playlist was last updated, above its name.
• Pages whose background image is missing show the one from TMDb when you have set your token, otherwise the blurred poster.
• Settings take your TMDb “API Read Access Token” (or API Key), named as on TMDb, and check it with TMDb before saving it; “App Language” opens the system page where Ondo's language is chosen.
