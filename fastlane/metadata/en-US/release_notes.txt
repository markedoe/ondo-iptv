• Mac: full screen, from the button, the F key or a double-click.
• AirPlay from the Mac now sends the video to your TV, not only the sound.
• Resume starts exactly where you stopped, and a pause made while a movie opens stays paused.
• Find subtitles online from the player: OpenSubtitles, SubDL and SubSource with your own key, Addic7ed for series without a key.
• Downloads that stall now say why (provider refusal, connection limit, nothing received) and what to do.
• Ondo now speaks Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic and Hindi, as well as English and French.
• My List is now a tab on iPhone, with your movies, series and channels in one place; your own lists are now called collections.
• Tap an actor or a director on a movie or series page to see their other movies and series in your playlist.
• Search can be filtered by release years, and the grids sort by release date.
• Hide a whole language or country in one go (category names that start with |PT|, FR:…): the categories added later follow. The filter button is on Movies, Series and Live TV.
• Awards and festival selections (Cannes, Oscars, Berlin, Venice, Césars…) on movie pages, an Award Winners row, and a search filter by festival or ceremony.
