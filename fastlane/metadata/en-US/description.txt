Ondo is a player only: it does not host, provide or sell any content or IPTV subscription. Use it only with sources you are authorised to watch.

Ondo is an elegant IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Add the M3U playlist or the Xtream Codes login given by your provider: its movies, series and channels become a clean, fast library with no ads.

MOVIES & SERIES
• Poster library, categories, sorting and instant search
• Rich details: plot, cast, rating, trailer, seasons and episodes
• Continue watching, resume where you left off, next episode automatically
• My List, favourites and custom lists
• Download movies and episodes to watch offline

A PLAYER THAT JUST WORKS
• Two engines: Apple’s player (HDR, Picture in Picture, AirPlay) and a VLC-based engine for MKV, AVI and TS
• Cast to your TV or projector with AirPlay or Chromecast
• Audio and subtitle tracks, external SRT/VTT subtitles, aspect ratio, speed, sleep timer, screen lock
• Clear error messages that say whether the provider, the network or the app is at fault

LIVE TV
• Channels, TV guide and replay when your provider offers it
• Favourites, programme reminders and multiview on iPad and Mac

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Profiles with their own favourites and history, optional PIN and kids profiles
• Parental control: lock adult categories behind a PIN

SOURCES
• M3U / M3U8 playlists (link or file), Xtream Codes, XSPF and JSON playlists
• Several playlists, automatic refresh, backups
