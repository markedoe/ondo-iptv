Ondo is a player only: it does not host, provide or sell any content or IPTV subscription. Use it only with sources you are authorised to watch.

Ondo is an elegant IPTV player for iPhone, iPad and Mac. Add the M3U playlist or the Xtream Codes login given by your provider: its movies, series and channels become a clean, fast library with no ads.

MOVIES & SERIES
• Poster library, categories, sorting and instant search
• Rich details: plot, cast, rating, trailer, seasons and episodes
• Continue Watching: playback resumes exactly where you stopped, and the next episode starts automatically
• My List, favourites and custom lists
• Download movies and episodes to watch offline; when a download stalls, Ondo tells you why in plain words (provider refusal, connection limit, nothing received)

A PLAYER THAT JUST WORKS
• Two engines: Apple’s player (HDR, Picture in Picture, AirPlay) and a VLC-based engine for MKV, AVI and TS
• Audio and subtitle tracks, SRT/VTT subtitle files, aspect ratio, speed, sleep timer, control lock
• Online subtitle search from the player: OpenSubtitles, SubDL and SubSource with your own key, Addic7ed for series without a key
• Clear error messages that say whether the provider, the network or the app is at fault

ON YOUR TV
• AirPlay from iPhone, iPad and Mac: the video goes to your TV, not only the sound
• Chromecast from iPhone and iPad

ON THE MAC
• One window per stream, full screen (button, F key or double-click), keyboard shortcuts and a Playback menu

LIVE TV
• Channels, TV guide and replay when your provider offers it: restart the current programme, watch a past one, pause live TV
• Favourites and programme reminders
• Multiview of up to 4 channels on iPad and Mac

FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Profiles with their own favourites and history, optional PIN and kids profiles
• Parental control: lock adult categories behind a PIN

SOURCES
• M3U / M3U8 playlists (link or file), Xtream Codes, XSPF and JSON playlists
• Several playlists, automatic refresh, backups

LANGUAGES
• English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic and Hindi

PRIVACY
• No account, no ads and no analytics of its own: your playlists and history stay on your device.
