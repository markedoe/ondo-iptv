Ondo is a player only: it does not host, provide or sell any content or IPTV subscription. Use it only with sources you are authorised to watch.

Ondo is an elegant IPTV player for Android phones, tablets and Android TV. Add the M3U playlist or the Xtream Codes login given by your provider: its movies, series and channels become a clean, fast library with no ads.

MOVIES & SERIES
• Featured carousel, rows by category, posters and instant search
• Rich details: plot, cast, rating, seasons and episodes
• Continue watching, resume where you left off, next episode
• My List for the titles you want to watch

A PLAYER THAT JUST WORKS
• Built on Android’s media player (HLS, MPEG-TS, MP4, MKV)
• Audio tracks and subtitles, Picture-in-Picture
• Clear error messages: provider, network or app
• Stream statistics: see whether a freeze comes from the network or the player

LIVE TV
• Channels by category with what is on now and next
• Channels in My List, a TV guide and programme reminders

ON ANDROID TV AND GOOGLE TV
• Every screen with the remote
• OK to pause, the arrows to go back and forward, CH+ / CH− and the number keys to change channel

SOURCES
• M3U / M3U8, XSPF and JSON playlists (link or file) and Xtream Codes accounts
• Several playlists, automatic refresh
• Adult categories stay hidden until you choose to show them

IN YOUR LANGUAGE
• English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Arabic and Hindi

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no tracking, no ads: your playlists stay on your device.

Ondo does not include any channel, playlist or subscription and is not affiliated with any IPTV provider.
